# Account franchise sync

The Firebase UID owns one D1 pc_franchises row. Authenticated GET/POST /api/multiplayer/franchise never accepts an owner UID from the client. Server revision compare-and-swap rejects stale writes; the previous committed JSON stays in previous_doc. Saves are bounded to 750,000 streamed request bytes, keeping both current and prior documents within the D1 row budget. Oversized saves remain local and show a sync error; they are not silently truncated. Transient replay simulations are excluded, while roster, team identity, unlocks, balances, playbook, depth, game progress, history and box scores persist.

The app gates editing on initial account hydration. It saves after 700ms of inactivity and retries/checks cloud every ten seconds while visible. Local caches are per UID; signing into a different account remounts the app. The legacy anonymous franchise is claimed once by the first account that loads it on that device, leaving its original key untouched. On the initial rollout, open the device with the desired team first and wait for Saved to your account. Then open the other device with the same phone login.

Two differing legacy/dirty saves require choosing account or device. No automatic roster, currency or stats union occurs. Both versions are backed up in the UID-specific local backups key (last three choices). A stale write rechecks cloud and asks again. Navigation warns while changes are unsaved. Local-storage failure shows a warning. This remains a client-authoritative friendly-game economy, not tamper-proof competitive progression.

Validation: native Worker/Durable Object/D1 tests cover owner separation, cross-client reload, revision advancement, stale update rejection and malformed roster writes. Pure sync tests cover first upload, clean downloads, two dirty saves and preservation of roster/box scores while removing replay frames. These checks do not claim testing a physical phone or two real Firebase sessions.
